AEON Creator Intelligence — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

AEON Creator Intelligence is an internal tool of the AEON Athletics brand, operated by Aeon Athletics Group, trgovina in storitve, d.o.o. (Aeon Athletics Group d.o.o.), Polje 9Z, 6310 Izola - Isola, Slovenia, registration number 8701580000, VAT ID SI10173960 ("we"). Privacy contact: luis@aeonathletics.com.

2. Data we obtain from Meta

  • About our staff who log in with Facebook Login for Business: Facebook user ID and name, the Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account they choose to share, the permissions granted and an access token.
  • About Instagram creators, through Meta's Instagram Creator Marketplace: username, account ID, biography, country, verification status, whether the creator joined the creator marketplace, follower count, account reach, and aggregated statistics (gender, age range, country, city, follower or non-follower) about the accounts that engaged with the creator's content. These are aggregated figures provided by Meta; we never receive data about individual followers.

3. Why we use it

Only to identify and evaluate Instagram creators for possible paid partnerships with the AEON Athletics brand, to check whether a creator's audience fits our markets, and to let authorised staff connect our own Facebook Page and Instagram account. The tool does not contact creators, publish content, sell data or use it for advertising targeting. Decisions about partnerships are taken by our staff.

4. Who can access it

Only authorised members of our marketing team. We do not sell this data.

Our staff use Anthropic's AI assistant when operating and reviewing the tool, so Anthropic may process this data while providing that service to us.

If we later provide Meta with a temporary version of the tool for its app review, that version will be hosted on Google Cloud in the EU (Belgium); the tool itself will keep data only in memory during a session of up to 30 minutes and will not save it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep creator results and technical request records only for as long as we need them to evaluate possible partnerships, and we delete them sooner if you ask us to (see section 6).

6. Your rights and data deletion

To request access to or deletion of your data, or to exercise any other data protection right, e-mail luis@aeonathletics.com. If you are a creator, include your Instagram username: we will delete every stored result and record that names your account and reply with a confirmation code. If you logged in to the tool with Facebook, you can also remove the app in your Facebook settings (Business integrations) and e-mail us to delete the data we hold about you. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
