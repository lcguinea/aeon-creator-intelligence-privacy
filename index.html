AEON Creator Intelligence — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 25 November 2026

2. Data we process

  • About our staff who connect the tool with Facebook Login for Business: Facebook user ID and name, the Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account they choose to share, the permissions granted and an access token.
  • About Instagram creators, through Meta's Instagram Creator Marketplace: username, account ID, biography, country, verification status, whether the creator joined the creator marketplace, follower count, account reach, interactions, and aggregated statistics (gender, age range, country, city, follower or non-follower) about the accounts that engaged with the creator's content. These are aggregated figures provided by Meta; we never receive data about individual followers.
  • Information our staff add in the tool: shortlists of creators, the status of a possible partnership and notes.
  • About our staff who use the tool: username, name, role, a one-way hash of their password and a record of the actions they take in the tool.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

Only to identify and evaluate Instagram creators for possible paid partnerships with the AEON Athletics brand, to check whether a creator's audience fits our markets, and to let authorised staff connect our own Facebook Page and Instagram account. The tool does not contact creators, publish content, sell data or use it for advertising targeting. Decisions about partnerships are taken by our staff.

We process creator data on the basis of our legitimate interest in finding suitable creators for brand partnerships (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). We use only the data Meta makes available through its Creator Marketplace and only what we need to assess a possible partnership. You can object at any time (see section 7). We process data about our staff to give them secure, individual access to the tool, also on the basis of our legitimate interest.

4. Who can access it

Only authorised members of our marketing team, each with a personal account and a role. Their actions are logged. We do not sell this data and we do not export it from the tool.

The tool does not send data to Anthropic or any other AI provider, and we do not use Anthropic's AI assistant to process data obtained from Meta when operating the tool. While the tool was being built and prepared for app review (November 2026), our staff used Anthropic's AI assistant, which processed some of this data, mainly identifiers of our own Facebook Page and Instagram account and Meta's test data. On 25 November 2026, the day the tool started receiving real data from Meta, the assistant also processed the results of a few test queries while a technical fault was being fixed: public profile data and aggregated statistics of 3 creators and the usernames of 5 creators. We removed that data from our development files the same day; since then the assistant works only with invented test data.

5. Where it is stored

The tool runs on Render (Render Services, Inc.) in Frankfurt, Germany, and stores its data in a database operated by Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) in Ireland. Both act as our processors under their data processing terms; under those terms data may also be processed outside the EU, including in the United States, with safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Access tokens are stored encrypted, all connections are encrypted, and the database cannot be read through Supabase's public data API, which is switched off.

The separate version of the tool that we provide to Meta for its app review is also hosted by Render in Frankfurt. It keeps data only in memory during a session of up to 30 minutes and does not save it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Creator profiles and statistics: deleted automatically when a creator has not been retrieved or viewed for 90 days and is not on an active shortlist. While a creator is on an active shortlist, we refresh the statistics from Meta about once a week; creators our staff viewed in the last 60 days are refreshed about once a month.
  • Earlier versions of a creator's statistics: 180 days.
  • Meta's original responses, kept as a technical record: 30 days.
  • Searches and their result lists: 90 days.
  • Record of staff actions: 365 days.
  • Daily copies of shortlists, statuses and notes, kept for recovery: the last 7 days.

We delete data sooner if you ask us to (see section 7).

7. Your rights and data deletion

To request access to or deletion of your data, to object to its use, or to exercise any other data protection right, e-mail luis@aeonathletics.com. If you are a creator, include your Instagram username: we will delete every stored result and record that names your account, including the daily copies, and reply with a confirmation code. We then keep only a one-way hash of your username and account ID, so that the tool can never retrieve your account again. If you logged in to the tool with Facebook, you can also remove the app in your Facebook settings (Business integrations) and e-mail us to delete the data we hold about you. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
